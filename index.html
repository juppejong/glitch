About

Why GLITCH exists

Built for people whose interests aren't a "general audience" — and who are tired of merch that treats them like one.

One niche, one design

Every print starts from a real subculture — modular synths, CTF culture, poetcore — not a generic mood board.

Print-on-demand

Nothing is pre-made or stocked. Your order is printed by Printify when you place it, which means no overproduction and no dead stock.

Small and direct

GLITCH is run lean, with new designs added as they're finished rather than on a seasonal calendar.
